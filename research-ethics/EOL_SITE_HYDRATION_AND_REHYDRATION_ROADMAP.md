# EOL simulation to site hydration and programmatic rehydration

**Public research status — 2026-11-10**

- **Stage 0: SITE HYDRATION / PUBLICATION — IN PROGRESS.**
- **Stage 1: STABLE PROGRAMMATIC CODE REHYDRATION — PLANNED; NOT ADMITTED.**
- **Scope:** publicly releasable theory, specifications, proof-of-concept records, and provenance-qualified artifacts. This document does not publish or describe private source material.

## Why this work is being published

The current site-hydration effort follows a series of controlled end-of-life (EOL) simulations. The earlier **EOL-under-duress** research examined preservation and responsible release of intellectual work under severe hypothetical constraints, including simulated code recovery from a hazardous environment. These were research scenarios, **not evidence that the hypothetical circumstances occurred**.

The governing distinction in the earlier EOL work was that the release of **project-owned publication holds** did not release security, integrity, privacy, provenance, consent, or third-party restrictions. Preserving knowledge did not authorize unrestricted disclosure. The work later developed into source recovery, reconstruction, validation, and program rehydration research.

The immediate task is to finish publishing a coherent, navigable, provenance-qualified public record before treating general-purpose automated code rehydration as a next-stage undertaking.

## Stage 0 — Site hydration and publication

**Current objective:** make eligible research accessible as properly linked, intelligible, separately qualified public material.

1. Recover and inventory the eligible corpus without silently rewriting original evidence.
2. Distinguish original source, recovered material, editorial reconstruction, generated candidates, and accepted successors.
3. Publish public theory, architecture, contracts, prototype demonstrations, and bounded validation reports with their known limitations.
4. Repair site/repository navigation, landing pages, cross-references, metadata, and publication-status notices.
5. Apply privacy, consent, intellectual-property, security, and third-party-rights screening **before** public placement.
6. Keep unresolved sources and missing artifacts explicitly unresolved; a page, checksum, or placeholder must not imply an available binary or a completed implementation.
7. Record the actual release identity, provenance, test boundary, and admission status per artifact.

**Stage 0 completion condition:** a reviewed, navigable public index and its eligible artifacts are published with accurate links, permissions, provenance, status, and proof ceilings. This condition has **not been declared satisfied** by this document.

## Stage 1 — Stable programmatic rehydration of code

**Target:** reconstruct programs from admissible evidence and explicit structural/semantic contracts using primarily deterministic methods and expert systems. This stage is **future work**, not an activated or fully verified general capability.

The intended evidence and construction order is:

1. **Original evidence first:** authenticated source bytes, historical revisions, manifests, provenance, and dependency closure where actually available.
2. **Programmatic reconstruction:** parsing, normalization, typed relationships, explicit rules, fixed transformations, and expert-system inference operating under declared constraints.
3. **Auditable assembly:** identify interfaces and dependencies, construct bounded artifacts, retain source-to-output traces, and expose omissions or conflicts.
4. **Verification and qualification:** reproducibility checks, contract checks, finite tests, independent inspection, and a separate admission decision. Passing a test does not itself grant authority or establish universal correctness.
5. **Synthetic generative text as a last resort only:** when evidence-bound methods cannot supply required material, any generative proposal is explicitly labeled **generated candidate**, never **recovered source**. Its use requires a separately justified scope, review, testing, and adoption decision. Unsupported inference must remain unresolved instead of silently inventing history.

### Required distinctions

- **Hydration of a public site is not code rehydration.**
- **Recovered** is not **reconstructed**; **reconstructed** is not **synthetically generated**.
- **Proposed**, **tested**, **verified within a scope**, **admitted**, and **authorized to execute** are separate states.
- An expert-system result is a rule-governed inference, not an independent source of historical truth.
- Unknown, conflicting, restricted, or absent material must remain visible and may result in a blocked outcome.
- No private dataset, unpublished protected record, credential, or security-sensitive operational detail is authorized for release by this roadmap.

## Publication and research ethics

The project aims to grow systems from declared functional seeds and inspectable evidence, rather than present synthetic prose as recovered engineering history. The research direction includes heuristic-grown seeds and recursive factories of factories, while retaining explicit control over construction, interpretation, validation, and release. The human operator remains the adoption and publication authority.

**This document is a public roadmap and historical summary, not a test attestation, runtime authorization, security certificate, or declaration of Stage 0 completion.**
