# AI Collaboration Attribution Protocol

Use ACAP to record how a human author and an AI collaborator worked together. Start with the [protocol form](protocol.md). The human author completes the **Human Author Declaration**. The AI collaborator uses the [AI entry point](ai-entry-point.md) to write its own **AI Generative Report**.

Use the Contribution Registry to record candidate material and the Adoption Registry to record what the human author chose to incorporate. The [paired disclosure example](examples/paired-disclosure.md) shows the two accounts and their supporting records.
