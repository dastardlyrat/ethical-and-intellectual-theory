# Cycles in Clay for Pottery of Delusion

*Cycles in Clay for Pottery of Delusion* is an illustrated collection of selected poetry organized through the language of pottery: sourcing, wedging, kneading, forming, manipulation, and pottery as finished work.

The collection explores trauma, intimacy, uncertainty, delusion, sleep disruption, self-destruction, religion, recovery, and the use of a machine mirror in interpretive work. It is presented here as creative writing and visual art, not as clinical evidence, independent verification, or a governing statement of present belief.
