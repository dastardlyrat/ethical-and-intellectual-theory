## Evidence and source discipline

The new published_position category is a summary of supplied public material, not a newly recovered direct quotation or independently verified personal fact. Its source_record_refs preserve the cited record’s status; confirmed commitments, source-scoped requirements, proposed methods, examples, and historical observations remain distinguishable.

The source-record identifiers and repository-relative citations describe the source material in this revision. Later changes to those documents require a renewed correspondence check. Private source locators, chat identifiers, precise personal dates, and input fingerprints are not disclosed. A public source entry is not independent attestation of its underlying private evidence.

Every dossier boundary case is hypothetical. The programming example is separately identified historical technical material. This reconstitution does not rerun its tests, generalize its local survivor choice, or convert its bounded results into a claim about personal conduct or general competence.

Production and publication approval do not establish acceptance of every editorial interpretation. The review-status fields state that distinction. The dossier does not grant authority for unrelated ingestion, transfer, personal modeling, prediction, imitation, authentication, or delegation.

## Presentation, privacy, and limits

The human dossier is generated from the structured values and checked for exact correspondence. All claim blocks, terminology, source roles, qualifications, relationships, cases, unresolved questions, and review statuses remain visible. Local document links and heading fragments are checked. Core Markdown headings, lists, and code spans avoid raw HTML anchors and pipe-table layouts.

The four changed files are screened as complete bytes for known identities/accounts, private paths and contacts, identity-bearing locators, precise dates, UUID forms, private source fingerprints, credential patterns, and replacement characters. The document remains de-identified, not guaranteed unrecognizable. Hosting accounts and the combination of ideas may still support associations.

Structural and source correspondence checks do not prove universal semantic equivalence, independent authorship, scientific validity, personal compliance, source authenticity, or psychological identity. No greater ethical theory, general profiling permission, universal technical policy, or runtime enforcement mechanism is inferred.

No screen capture, screen sharing, UI automation, dependency installation, or runtime replay is performed. Native rendered-preview behavior remains unverified. Publication uses normal history-preserving commits with verified no-reply author and committer identities; exact remote file readback is retained in local publication receipts.
