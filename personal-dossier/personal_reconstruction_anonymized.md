## Purpose and limits

**purpose:** Provide a readable and structured account of declared ethical positions and their relationships.

**source boundary:** The supplied doctrine statements and the associated conversation, approvals, dossier, and consistency review.

**method:** Combine the two dossier parts, apply the accepted review corrections, paraphrase distinctive wording, and retain evidential distinctions.

**identity policy:** Use no personal name, account, source locator, precise date, or persistent person identifier. Record and source IDs are local to this document.

**authority:** Production of this edition was explicitly approved. This record does not itself authorize external publication, transfer, prediction, imitation, authentication, or delegation.

**limit:** The account describes declared positions, not a complete person, independently verified conduct, or a unique cognitive method.


## De-identification

**Classification:** De-identified; irreversible anonymity is not established.

## Evidence categories

- **direct_declaration:** A position expressed directly by the individual in the source conversation.
- **supplied_position:** A position contained in supplied text; inclusion does not independently establish authorship of every sentence.
- **assistant_formulation:** Wording or organization produced by the assistant.
- **scoped_approval:** Approval of a described activity or interpretation, limited to its stated scope.
- **editorial_interpretation:** A relationship or explanation drawn from sources and kept distinct from a direct declaration.
- **unresolved:** A question for which the record does not establish a responsible conclusion.

## Declared positions and approved interpretations

### CLM-01

Permission to examine intellectual work does not automatically include permission to create an attributable profile of its creator.

**Evidence class:** supplied_position

**Sources:** SRC-01, SRC-04

**Interpretive status:** Supported position in supplied documents.

Limits:

- Does not automatically classify every instance of pattern recognition as personal profiling.

### CLM-02

Explicit permission may authorize a defined activity; a material expansion requires renewed permission.

**Evidence class:** direct_declaration

**Sources:** SRC-02, SRC-03, SRC-04

**Interpretive status:** Direct clarification developed in subsequent documents.

Limits:

- Does not establish permission beyond the activity actually approved.

### CLM-03

Crediting a contribution and obtaining permission serve different obligations; neither automatically substitutes for the other.

**Evidence class:** supplied_position

**Sources:** SRC-01, SRC-04

**Interpretive status:** Supported doctrinal distinction.

Limits:

- Does not determine ownership or dependence in an individual dispute.

### CLM-04

Statements, observations, inferences, corroboration, and attestations must remain distinguishable, with claims limited to what their evidence establishes.

**Evidence class:** supplied_position

**Sources:** SRC-04

**Interpretive status:** Supported standard for evidence handling.

Limits:

- Classification alone does not make a claim accurate or verified.

### CLM-05

A retained account of a person does not acquire authority to override that person's present instruction or correction.

**Evidence class:** supplied_position

**Sources:** SRC-04

**Interpretive status:** Supported limit on personal modeling.

Limits:

- Does not resolve every conflict involving identity, context, prior constraints, or third-party interests.

### CLM-06

Similar expression can result from shared conventions, task constraints, or coincidence; resemblance alone does not settle derivation.

**Evidence class:** direct_declaration

**Sources:** SRC-05, SRC-06

**Interpretive status:** Direct position with an approved qualification: the relevant construction set is task-constrained, not a claim that language has only finitely many possible expressions.

Limits:

- No probability of coincidence is established.
- A claim of coincidence does not establish independence.

### CLM-07

An instruction excluding material establishes a boundary but does not independently prove that a resulting output has no dependence on that material.

**Evidence class:** scoped_approval

**Sources:** SRC-05, SRC-06

**Interpretive status:** Approved interpretation of the distinction between a restriction and demonstrated compliance.

Limits:

- Does not establish that a particular system accessed a particular source.
- The meaning of restricted implementation material depends on the governing context.

### CLM-08

The individual affirms an ethical commitment while acknowledging that sustained interest may precede recognition of a boundary crossing.

**Evidence class:** direct_declaration

**Sources:** SRC-05, SRC-06

**Interpretive status:** Generalized declaration preserving commitment and delayed recognition while omitting sensitive personal context.

Limits:

- Does not establish perfect conduct, absence of responsibility, or an independent explanation of behavior.

### CLM-09

When unauthorized reliance is identified, the response should include stopping that reliance and addressing affected work within the available authority.

**Evidence class:** scoped_approval

**Sources:** SRC-05, SRC-06

**Interpretive status:** Approved interpretation of removal and redress.

Limits:

- Suspected influence, known dependence, and established unauthorized use remain different statuses.
- A declaration of removal does not establish complete removal.
- Does not authorize destruction of records or changes to another person's work.

### CLM-10

Non-coercive transfers should remain open to voluntary clarification, negotiation, and remedy.

**Evidence class:** scoped_approval

**Sources:** SRC-05, SRC-06

**Interpretive status:** Approved interpretation of the individual's commitment to mediation.

Limits:

- Does not guarantee participation, agreement, ownership, transfer authority, or legal effectiveness.

## Relationships among positions

These relationships are editorial interpretations, not additional personal declarations.

### REL-01: Commitment and recognition

A continuing ethical commitment can be recorded alongside delayed recognition. The authorization, event, awareness, and response still require separate assessment.

**Related claims:** CLM-08, CLM-09, CLM-13

**Limit:** No general threshold for responsibility or adequate remedy is established.

### REL-02: Convergence and dependence

Possible convergence limits unsupported accusations, while known dependence still requires honest treatment.

**Related claims:** CLM-03, CLM-06, CLM-07

**Limit:** Neither resemblance nor a claim of coincidence resolves a particular case alone.

### REL-03: Removal and correction history

Stopping reliance and retaining an accountable correction record may serve different purposes and require distinct permissions.

**Related claims:** CLM-09, CLM-12

**Limit:** The need for accountability does not authorize duplication of restricted material.

### REL-04: Permission and mediation

A later agreement may set future terms or address an earlier dispute without establishing that permission existed earlier.

**Related claims:** CLM-02, CLM-10

**Limit:** Agreement about future use need not settle all historical claims.

### REL-05: Adaptation and authority

A personal account may assist communication while remaining subordinate to explicit instructions and the limits of its authorization.

**Related claims:** CLM-05, CLM-11, CLM-14

**Limit:** It cannot grant itself authority to act for the person.


## Hypothetical boundary cases

Every case below is hypothetical and makes no claim about personal history.

### CASE-01: Recognition after a change in analytical purpose

**Scenario:** A reviewer permitted to examine a work begins constructing a creator profile and later notices that profiling was excluded.

**Assessment:** Stop and assess the affected activity. Delayed recognition does not determine whether the earlier activity was authorized or what remedy is sufficient.

**Unresolved:** The timing of awareness and the proportionate remedy require case-specific evidence.

**Related claims:** CLM-01, CLM-08, CLM-09

### CASE-02: Resemblance after source exclusion

**Scenario:** An output resembles a short construction in material that was excluded from the task.

**Assessment:** Record the resemblance and restriction separately. Examine competing explanations without treating either coincidence or a violation as established.

**Unresolved:** The available evidence may not resolve derivation.

**Related claims:** CLM-06, CLM-07

### CASE-03: Replacement with a correction record

**Scenario:** An author replaces an identified unauthorized contribution and keeps a bounded explanation of the correction.

**Assessment:** Assess permission for the replacement and the correction record separately. Do not claim universal removal without support.

**Unresolved:** Retention, access, and any claim of replacement independence remain context-dependent.

**Related claims:** CLM-09, CLM-12

### CASE-04: Disputed voluntary transfer

**Scenario:** Two parties agree an exchange was voluntary but disagree about the uses it permitted.

**Assessment:** Clarify the disputed scope and consider future agreement without rewriting the chronology of permission.

**Unresolved:** Participation, settlement terms, and authority to agree have not been established.

**Related claims:** CLM-02, CLM-10

### CASE-05: An inferred preference conflicts with a request

**Scenario:** A system expects a detailed response, but the individual requests brevity.

**Assessment:** The inferred preference does not independently authorize overriding the current request. Expose material ambiguity rather than resolving it invisibly through a profile.

**Unresolved:** More consequential conflicts may require clarification of identity, context, or prior explicit constraints.

**Related claims:** CLM-05, CLM-14
