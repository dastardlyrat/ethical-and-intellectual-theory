# Voluntary Participation, Consent, and Correction

This is **proposed guidance**, not a completed consent record. No ReShade maintainer, shader author, or community member has been enrolled or represented as an endorser.

## Welcome

Feedback on the published text, corrections, objections to AI, reproducibility suggestions, and better non-AI engineering tools are welcome when offered voluntarily.

## Not presumed

- Public access, silence, a reply, or a repository license is not consent for personal methodological profiling.
- Consent to read something is not consent to republish, retain, train a model on, or transfer it.
- An individual's refusal is not a technical hurdle to route around through another account, intermediary, or medium.
- A critic's contribution is not endorsement of this research program.

## Before any private review

Agree separately on purpose, exactly which **authorized** material will be shared, recipients, whether model processing is allowed, quoted outputs, retention duration, access restrictions, and prospective withdrawal/correction procedures. Record the actual opt-in; do not infer it.

Screen for third-party code, private chat passages, personal information, and credentials. Exclude material that cannot be properly disclosed. The public repository is **not** suitable for posting secrets, nonpublic conversations, or third-party source.

## Corrections

When a boundary error appears, stop the affected use, establish what happened, document the limits of the evidence, correct or remove affected content within authorized control, and record any unresolved copies or consequences. Later consent must not be retroactively represented as permission that existed earlier.

No invitation, private conversation link, or access to someone else's materials is issued by this page.
