## Record 02

**Three regretted experiences and a change in priority**

These are self-reported experiences and retrospective judgments. They preserve my assessment of the work and interaction without claiming independent verification of another participant's intentions.

### 1. Requested approach and investigative direction

In work involving a repository fork and code transmigration through a CPU reference, an author requested the use of a frontier model. I instead pursued tooling and a bug hunt. I regret the divergence between the requested approach and the direction I took.

### 2. Depth-oracle construction and unmet requirements

While researching ReShade requirements and services, I developed a depth oracle around needs I was trying to satisfy. In retrospect, I should have examined whether the existing work associated with `DisplayDepth.fx` could be forked, and limited new construction to requirements that it did not meet. The availability of a suitable fork remains conditional.

### 3. An unsuccessful opening

I approached an unfamiliar but recognizable participant intending to discuss community needs and API use. I perceived the opening exchange as a rebuke of my work. The conversation never reached those subjects because I panicked. My increasingly frantic attempts to communicate further degraded the situation. A subsequent attempt to establish veracity received no reply, leaving that validation unresolved.

### Change in priority

Consent moved to the top of my priorities when approaching an unfamiliar participant under adverse conditions. This records a lesson I now emphasize; it does not establish what another participant intended or settle every question about the earlier interactions.
