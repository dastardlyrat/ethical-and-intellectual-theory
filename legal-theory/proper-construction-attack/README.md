# Proper Construction Attack — lawful means

**Author-supplied definition:** **An attack carried through legal and lawful means.**

## Scope

**Proper Construction Attack** is a proposed term within the author's legal-theory research for an adversarial action or course of action conducted through means that are legally permitted under the applicable law.

The term describes the **legal status of the means** as proposed in the definition. It does **not** establish that any actual conduct is lawful, that its ultimate purpose or effects are lawful, that harm is absent, or that the action is morally or ethically acceptable. Those questions require separate examination of the specific facts, jurisdiction, and legal rules.

In this terminology, *attack* is an analytical descriptor for an adversarial act; its use is **not** an allegation of a criminal offense or a finding of legal liability. *Proper construction* is part of the author's chosen name and is not a judicial approval or certification.

## Relation to other terminology

- **[Legal Crime](../legal-crime/README.md)** concerns conduct judged morally or ethically repulsive and abhorrent while legal. It is **not automatically equivalent** to a Proper Construction Attack: neither term, by definition alone, establishes the other's conditions.
- **[Witness-failure terminology](../witness-failure-terminology/README.md)** identifies defects in legal justificatory ancestry and dependency; it is a separate diagnostic category.

## Publication boundary

This definition is conceptual only. It supplies no operational method, target, tactic, or legal authorization to undertake an attack.
