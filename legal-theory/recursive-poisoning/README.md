# Recursive Poisoning

**Author-supplied definition:** The **need to ingest the poison in order to understand the material knowledge or Anti-Knowledge**.

## Scope

**Recursive Poisoning** names the author's proposed examination problem: understanding material knowledge or Anti-Knowledge may require first encountering or ingesting the very poisoned material under examination.

Here, *ingest* is retained from the author's conceptual definition. This page does **not** establish that ingestion is universally necessary, harmless, or sufficient for understanding, and does not recommend performing an unsafe operation.

The defining issue is the asserted dependency of **inspection upon exposure to the subject being inspected**. Whether a particular case demonstrates this dependency remains an open evidentiary question.

## Status and limits

**Status:** Author-defined research vocabulary; proposed epistemic problem, not a proven universal requirement or operational capability. The classification does not by itself authorize exposure, adoption, propagation, or deployment of harmful material.
